# five-harness-investigation Specification

## Purpose

Defines the first five real CLI Targets and makes their topic investigations visible without confusing research progress, unreviewed candidates, and published capability facts.

## Requirements

### Requirement: First-wave chapter catalog
Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode, Pi and OMP SHALL remain distinct product identities, each addressed by its catalog product id, so the product formerly named `codex-cli` is `codex`. Each product SHALL publish seven current topic chapters using its own fixed official sources, covering its declared surfaces, with all fixed question IDs and surfaces accounted for and no unsupported package-version extrapolation.

#### Scenario: Five-product release
- **WHEN** first-wave production knowledge is published
- **THEN** the release has 35 current product-topic chapters and 53 question states per product, with Pi and OMP kept separate and Codex addressed by product id `codex`
